### How it works

1. **Create a payment link** in the dashboard, or a charge through the API.
2. **Your customer pays** on a hosted checkout, at a rate locked for them, to an address made for that payment alone.
3. **You get paid.** Once the payment is confirmed on-chain, it is forwarded to your own wallet within minutes.

### For developers

- A REST API with idempotency keys
- Signed webhooks (HMAC-SHA256) for every charge and invoice event, with retries and a delivery log
- Test mode, to try every flow without real money
- An [MCP server](https://velirapay.com/docs/mcp), so an AI assistant can raise invoices, make payment links and check what has been paid
