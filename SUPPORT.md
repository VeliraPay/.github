# Getting help

- **Using VeliraPay:** the [documentation](https://velirapay.com/docs) covers the dashboard, the API, webhooks and the libraries.
- **Your account, a payment or a payout:** email contact@velirapay.com from the address your account uses. Please don't post account or payment details in a GitHub issue.
- **Whether the service is up:** [status.velirapay.com](https://status.velirapay.com).
- **A bug in a library:** open an issue in that library's repository.
- **A vulnerability:** see the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
